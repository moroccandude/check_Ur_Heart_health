age: The age of the patient in years.

sex: The gender of the patient.

1 = Male
0 = Female
cp (Chest Pain Type): Describes the type of chest pain experienced.

0 = Typical angina
1 = Atypical angina
2 = Non-anginal pain
3 = Asymptomatic
trestbps (Resting Blood Pressure): The resting blood pressure in mmHg (millimeters of mercury).

chol (Serum Cholesterol): Serum cholesterol level in mg/dL.

fbs (Fasting Blood Sugar): Indicates if fasting blood sugar is greater than 120 mg/dL.

1 = True (High blood sugar)
0 = False (Normal blood sugar)
restecg (Resting Electrocardiographic Results): Results from an ECG test.

0 = Normal
1 = ST-T wave abnormality
2 = Left ventricular hypertrophy
thalach (Maximum Heart Rate Achieved): The highest heart rate (beats per minute) achieved during exercise.

exang (Exercise-Induced Angina): Indicates if the patient experienced chest pain during exercise.

1 = Yes
0 = No
oldpeak: ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest. Measures the difference in heart electrical activity before and after exercise.

slope: The slope of the peak exercise ST segment.

0 = Upsloping
1 = Flat
2 = Downsloping

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Symptoms Based on These Features:
Chest pain (especially typical angina).
Shortness of breath (low thalach, high oldpeak).
High blood pressure or cholesterol (trestbps, chol).
Exercise intolerance (exang, thalach).
Abnormal ECG readings (restecg, slope).
Pain worsening with exertion (oldpeak, exang).
Previous heart issues (ca, thal).
